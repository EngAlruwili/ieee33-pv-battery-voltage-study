## Repository description

Reproducible Python/pandapower study of voltage profiles and energy-loss tradeoffs in the IEEE 33-bus feeder with solar PV and battery storage, including SOC accounting, validation checks and sensitivity analysis.

## Short project description

**Voltage Regulation and Energy Loss Reduction in a Distribution Network Using Solar PV and Battery Storage** is a simulation-based undergraduate project prepared for Abdullah Alruwaili, an Electrical and Electronics Engineering student at the University of Jeddah specializing in Power Engineering. It compares the original IEEE 33-bus feeder with PV-only and PV-plus-storage cases using synthetic hourly profiles. The package emphasizes reproducible load flow, explicit battery energy accounting and transparent interpretation of voltage and loss tradeoffs. The prepared materials require Abdullah's review and local reproduction before being represented as completed personal work.
